# 02 — The Twelve Axes

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — v0.01. Each axis is a realm and a mechanic.
> The axes are the fabric of the plane; scenarios live in them.

The twelve axes from the Kanon (see `CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` and the corpus). Each
axis is a **realm** (a space) and a **mechanic** (how that space behaves). They
are the raw registers the scenarios are written in.

## The axes as realms

Each axis is a possible **realm** a scenario can take place in:

- **prehod** — the threshold between two spaces; doors, transitions.
- **beseda** — where words are uttered and have force; the trade.
- **um** — the inward hall of reflection.
- **smisel** — the wound-pattern; orientation.
- **značaj** — the trial of consistency.
- **prostor** — the open field of exchange.
- **čas** — the corridor of duration; Time's own house.
- **telo** — the felt-space; the body's register.
- **tišina** — the hushed chamber; the precondition.
- **pot** — the way; endurance.
- **resnica** — the court of verification.
- **dvom** — the crucible; where belief is tested.

## How axes + scenarios compose

A scenario names an axis (or a crossing of axes) and stages a mechanic within
it. Example: *"beseda × prehod"* — a word that, when spoken across a threshold,
changes the nature of the space it enters. The scenes built from such scenarios
are the playable moments.
