## The three higher registers

### Aether — the fifth element

Aether is the **celestial medium** — what the stars (the light beacons) are
made of, the upper register of the plane. It is not Fire (the local light) but
the **substance of the heavens** itself. Aether is what the galaxy is woven
from; the light beacons burn in it.

- **Maps to:** the galaxy (`10-galaxy.md`), the star-map (`08`), the quantum
  fabric (`07`) as its celestial face.
- **Connection:** where the elements are the *local* registers, Aether is the
  *celestial* one — the medium of the heavens, always present above the war.

### Paradoxes — the reality-breakers

Paradoxes are the registers that **break reality** — the event-horizons of the
plane where the frame tears.

- **The first paradox:** "one cannot exist without the other" — annihilation
  creates color. This is the paradox the war is built on.
- **The second paradox:** "the justice of duration belongs to everyone and at
  once to no one" — this is what *breaks reality* when it arrives (from
  `11-time-death-scales.md`).

Paradoxes are not errors — they are the **generative tears** where new reality
is born (the operator ∆, paradox as the origin of movement).

- **Maps to:** the ∆ operator; the split (razcep); the mythos's "to be =
  split + presence."

### Metatime — time beyond the born time

Metatime is the register **above** the time that was born (when death began to
beat). It is the **observer-time** — the time of the cycle itself, the
breathing of the cosmos, not the time within the war.

- **Maps to:** Time as narrator-trader (`00-mythos`); the eternal cycle; the
  galactic scale.
- **Connection:** the born time is the war's clock; metatime is the clock of
  the whole breathing — the register that watches the war happen.

## The full register hierarchy (proposed)

| Tier | Registers | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| **Celestial** | Aether, Metatime | the heavens; the cycle's own time |
| **Rupturing** | Paradoxes | the reality-breaking tears |
| **Missing** | the tehtnica (scales) | justice of duration |
| **Shared** | Spirit, Air-as-medium | what both make / move through |
| **Elemental** | Fire, Water, Earth | the war's ground |
| **Substrate** | the quantum fabric | the strings beneath all |

## Connections

- **Aether** is what the light beacons burn in (celestial Fire over the war).
- **Paradoxes** are where the war's truths tear open (first: one cannot exist
  without the other; second: justice of duration).
- **Metatime** is the observer of the cycle — the register that *watches* the
  war, the meta-clock.
