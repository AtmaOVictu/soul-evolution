# 09 — Earth (the third playable element)

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — v0.01. Beyond Light (Fire) and Dark (Air),
> there is **Earth** — the third playable element. Earth is what Time made
> when it breathed time into clay. Its scenario: **play as Earth against the
> forces that engulf you.**

## The origin of Earth

Before Time, light was aftershock — directionless, repeating. Time's first
acts were:
1. **trade the sand** (the dust resource, the granular residue),
2. **breathe time into clay** — and the clay became **Earth**.

So Earth is the **clay given life by Time**. It is the stable ground, the
material that Fire and Air move across and fight over. It is the middle that
both forces try to engulf.

*(ref: the origin myth — "before time used the sand traded and given to
breathe time into clay.")*

## Earth as a playable perspective

The player may align as **Earth** — not Light, not Dark, but the ground both
sweep over. Earth's character:

- **does not illuminate** (not Fire) — it does not add light,
- **does not conceal** (not Air) — it does not cast the same shadow,
- **holds** — it is stable, resistant, the material itself,
- **grounds the light** — it stops or steadies the sweeping aftershock-light
  (see `08-star-map.md`).

Aligning as Earth sets the player as the **defender** — the one the engulfing
forces press against.

## The scenario: Earth against the forces that engulf

The third scenario (after playing Fire, then Air) is to play **as Earth**,
holding ground while the forces surround you. The mechanic is **resistance and
steadiness**: Fire seeks to consume and lift you; Air seeks to erode and
scatter you. Earth endures by being the ground — by holding, not by striking.

## Earth's treasure and system (open)

Following `05-treasures.md` and `06-systems.md`, Earth should have a designated
treasure and a player-interactive system. Hypothesis:
- **Treasure:** the **ground** — stability, the held clay (the opposite of
  dust's formlessness: dust is unheld, Earth holds).
- **System:** **Steadiness / Grounding** — Earth holds, resists, and roots the
  light. Player interaction: hold ground, steady the field, endure the engulf.

## The three playable elements

| Element | Nature | Treasure | System |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** (Light) | illuminates, reveals | revealed light | Illumination (map/star) |
| **Air** (Dark) | conceals, ambushes | silence | Concealment / Repulsion |
| **Earth** (Clay) | holds, endures | the ground (stability) | Steadiness / Grounding |
