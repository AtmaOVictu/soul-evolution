# 05 — The Treasures (designated currencies)

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — v0.01. The principle: every entity has a
> designated treasure/currency — what it values, hoards, and trades. This is
> the economy of the "art to be hoarded, upgraded, traded," made entity-specific.
> It is also the concrete mechanism for "light and dark notice only what is
> theirs": each entity can perceive and use only its own treasure.

## The principle

Every entity of the plane carries a **designated treasure/currency** — the thing
it values above all, hoards across the cycle, and trades (brokered by Time).
No entity can spend another's currency; each is blind to the others' treasures
until Time facilitates the perception.

This is the economic ground of the mythos:
- the **windows** (frames featuring art) are each entity's treasure-hoard;
- **Time** (the trader) brokers exchange *between* currencies;
- the **blindness** means you can only perceive and use your own treasure.

## The mapping (hypothesis)

| Entity | Designated treasure/currency | What it is | Grounding |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** (Light/White) | **Revealed light** (✡ traces) | bright moments, truths made visible | "Sled je svetloba odnosa" — the trace is the light of relation |
| **Air** (Dark/Black) | **Silence / absence** (¬) | the held breath, the hollow | "evil... arises in his silence" (zlo.md) |
| **Neutron** (grey) | **Inertia / weight** | the heaviness of the broken | "movement grows heavy" |
| **Dust** | **Formlessness** | buildable potential | the character-builder currency |
| **Time** | **Duration** | the age's remaining moments | "the only true value is time" (txt.md) |
| **Spirit** (both) | **The shared register** | what neither alone produces | "both make spirit, always present" |
| **Player soul** | **Depends on alignment** | fire-currency or air-currency | the alignment doctrine |

## How the trade works

Time is the only entity that can handle all currencies. It brokers:
- **Fire ↔ Air** — revealed light for silence (and vice versa). This is the
  price of perception: to see the other's value, you must offer your own.
- **Neutron → Dust** — the broken broken further into formless potential.
- **Dust → build** — the player spends dust (their own if aligned,
  or traded) to build the soul.

The blindness is economic as well as perceptual: a white soul cannot spend
air-currency, and may not even see that black souls carry it. Trade (via Time)
is the only bridge.

## The player's designated treasure

The player's soul has a designated treasure determined by **alignment**:
- Aligned **White/Fire** → treasure is **revealed light** (✡). White actions
  are "good" because they align with what the player can perceive and spend.
- Aligned **Black/Air** → treasure is **silence** (¬).
- The **"better"** path (harmonizing both) gains access to **Spirit's shared
  register** — the one currency neither side hoards alone.

This makes "good / better / detriment" (scenario 008) concrete: an act is
**good** if it grows your own treasure, **better** if it touches Spirit's shared
register, a **detriment** if it spends a treasure you cannot perceive or hold.

## Tie to the windows

Each entity's treasure is displayed in a **window** — a frame featuring its art.
Hoarding = growing the treasure. Upgrading = deepening it. Trading = exchanging
it via Time. The windows are the visible face of the economy.
