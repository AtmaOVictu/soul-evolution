## The usable content (mapped into canon)

**The world-tree (axis mundi):**
> "The world tree or cosmic mountain appears in myths of almost all peoples:
> the Siberian shaman's birch, the Indian bodhi tree, the Mayan ceiba, the
> biblical tree in Eden. This is an **archetype of connection** — all cultures
> imagined some **center of the world where heaven and earth, gods and humans
> meet.**"

- Connects to the **Zemlja (Earth) axis** — the firm ground, the rootedness.
- The world-tree is the *center where the registers meet* — the point where the
  plane connects to the above (god) and below (earth). This is the OSEBNIK's
  center and the Council's axis.

**The Great Mother (goddess of life):**
> Anahita, Demetra, Kuan Yin, Devi, Mati Maria — the universal image of
> Mother Earth "who gives life and mercy: care, healing power, compassion."

- Connects to the Earth register as the life-giving, healing, merciful face.

**The Kurent (Slovenian carnival demon):**
> "A benevolent demon of fertility, somewhat like a Slovenian Dionysus, who
> with leaping and ringing revives the spring rebirth of nature."

- A named local (Slovenian) figure — spring rebirth, transformation.

## Key connections

- **The world-tree is the connection-center of the plane.** The star-map
  (08-star-map) — the constellations — hang from the world-tree; the Earth is
  its trunk. The axis mundi is where the registers meet.
- **The Great Mother is the Earth register's archetype** — the life-giving,
  merciful face of the firm ground, opposed to the consuming maw.
- **The harmonization of opposites** (from the reading): "to unite the light of
  knowledge with the dignity of character" — the Fire/Water, masculine/feminine
  harmonization is the soul's mission. This recurs as the "one cannot exist
  without the other" doctrine.
