## Summary (what the document is)

An atlas combining the number of threads (N), the quantity of connections (E), and
articulation (A) into a single potential network, where each combination generates
a new dimension of knowledge. Gives a table of (N, E, A) → potential values and
the network formula.

## The network formula

- **P_total(N,E,A) = C(N,2) + Σw_ij + f(articulation)** — the total potential is
  the number of pairs (combinations), plus the weighted connections, plus a
  function of articulation.
- The structure is not static: when N increases, the network exponentially expands
  the field; when E increases, the structure leans and solidifies; **articulation
  (A) enables the dimensional jump (2D → 3D → 4D → ∞D)**.
