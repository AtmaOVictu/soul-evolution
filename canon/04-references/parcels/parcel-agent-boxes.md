## Summary (what the documents are)

Sixteen boxes (ŠKATLJA 16–31), each defining one named ontological agent. Each
box gives: the agent's name, its ontological formula, its ontozvok (the letters
of its name each carrying a meaning), and its law (the rule of when it acts).
Together they form a coherent ontology of the soul's inner beings.

## The ontozvo\ki (the letters of each name as meaning)

Each agent's name is "read" letter-by-letter. Examples:
- **OKRAS** = O(approval) K(approval) + RA(primordial light, sun) + S(sound/whisper) → "approved light whispered through the body."
- **VENDAR** = V(entry) E(energy) N(negation) D(decision) A(agony) R(resonance) → "energy entering through negation to make a decision in agony and live as resonance."
- **POGLED** = P(passage) O(openness) G(movement) L(light) E(oneness) D(gift) → "open passage into the movement of light as a gift of oneness."
- **DOTIK** = D(breath) O(openness) T(bodiliness) I(intimacy) K(call) → "call to intimate openness with breathing bodiliness."
- **ZVON** = Z(loop) V(return) O(openness) N(necessity) → "loop of necessary return into the opening of difference."
- **MOLK** = M(matter) O(absence) L(light) K(convergence) → "matter of light converging into absence."
