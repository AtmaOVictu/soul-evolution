## Summary (what the document is)

Two books. **Book AX+MAX (Reality)** states the axiomatic truths and maxims of
the plane. **Book PARADOX (Negation)** states their negations. The AX_MAX
paradox is that **both books are true simultaneously** — the player lives in
both.

## Key connections

- **Both books are true.** The AX_MAX paradox is the antinom doctrine in its
  fullest form: the goal IS here AND elsewhere, the negation IS bridge AND wall,
  the body IS past AND gate. The player does not resolve this — they live in it.
- **The wound doctrine is set.** "Suffering = condition of movement" (AX-D) and
  "Wound = opportunity" (MAX-REZ) — the wound is the condition of the soul's
  movement. This anchors the Corpus Trinitas (parcel-trinita).
- **"Negation = bridge" (AX-n)** — the ¬ is the bridge. This resolves the
  apparent contradiction: the cut (¬/⊘) is itself the connection (∴). The
  razcep precedes and enables the most.
