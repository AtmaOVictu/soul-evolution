## Summary (what the document is)

A mandala defining evil and mapping the four elements (Fire/Air/Water/Stone) to

Collective / Capital / Nature / Technology / Idea).

## The core definition

> **"IDEA EVIL = E O" — EVIL AS (the integral of the past).**

- **Evil is the integral of the past** — evil is the accumulated residue of
  what has passed, uncut, unprocessed. This is the ℜ (residue) axis made
  absolute: when the past is not cut (¬) and bridged (∴), it accumulates as E/O.

## The elements' corrupted forms (selection)

| Element | Corrupted form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| FIRE (war) | ego-explosion, mob | fire's aggression, unbounded |
| AIR (illusion of freedom) | promise of freedom, fanaticism | air's deception |
| WATER (post-ascetic erosion) | passive multitude, erosion | water's dissolution |
| STONE (paralysis of institutions) | captivity, dogma | stone's rigidity |

- Each element, uncut, becomes its evil form: fire→war, air→illusion, water→
  erosion, stone→paralysis. The **cut** (¬/⊘) is what prevents the element from
  becoming its evil.

## Key connections

- **Evil = the integral of the past.** The zlo axis is accumulated residue —
  what is not cut and sealed (ℜ). This is the neutron/grey (the unresolved) made
  absolute. The cut (razcep) is the anti-evil.
- **The elements have corrupted forms.** Fire→war, Air→illusion, Water→erosion,
  Stone→paralysis. The canonical doctrine (parcel-optikon) is the uncorrupted
  form; the mandala of evil is the corrupted form. Both are the same element,
  cut or uncut.
- **The cut prevents evil.** Each corruption is the element *without* the cut
  (¬/⊘). The player's cut is what keeps fire from becoming war.
