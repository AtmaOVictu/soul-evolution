## Summary (what the document is)

A compendium of the double operator "le" (Slovenian: *only, merely*) as a
formal operator with two modes: **LE[samó]** (scope — narrows the range) and
**LE[ética]** (ethical ratification — grants legitimacy). Includes metrics,
antinom pairs, rules, and moves. Also defines the **word-plasticity vector**.

The word-vector has 9 dimensions: **Φ, Σ, Π, ⊥, ⧖, □, ∮, ⊢, ⟡** — the full
operator set (change, self, possibility, body, time, boundary, ethics, truth,

9-dimensional vector, not a single attribute. *(This expands `parcel-optikon`'s

## Key connections

- **The "le" operator IS the cut.** LE[samó] (only/merely) is the ¬/⊘ cut that
  limits scope — the razcep. LE[ética] ratifies (the good act). The maxim:
  "Without bridge (σ_B), 'le' is an excuse; with bridge, it is clarity." This
  is the alignment + treasury doctrine: limitation is only good when bridged.
- **δ_D (el diablo)** — the diabolos that unravels meaning from responsibility
  is the **opponent** — the misalignment. When δ_D rises and λ_LE falls, abuse.
- **The word-vector is the full word-protocol.** ψ_w ⟨Φ,Σ,Π,⊥,⧖,□,∮,⊢,⟡⟩ is
  the 9-dimension form of every word/soul — expanding the OPTIKON lexicon.
