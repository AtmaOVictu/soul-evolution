## Summary (what the document is)

A "surface dictionary" mapping ~50 themes (FIRE, WATER, AIR, EARTH, TRUTH,
LOVE, DOUBT, FAITH, HOPE, DESPAIR, FREEDOM, NECESSITY, ORDER, CHAOS, LIGHT...) to
a breath cycle (e.g. 4-4-4, 5-0-5), a gesture (NIH, RS, DN, PD, PU...), and a
voice (JA clear, ME soft, RE sharp, E repeat, OD silence).

## The usable pattern (mapped into canon)

Each theme is a **rhythm signature** — a breath cycle + gesture + voice. Examples:

| Theme | Breath | Voice | Maps to |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIRE / WATER / AIR / EARTH | 4-4-4 | JA | the four elements |
| TRUTH / RIGHT / GOOD | 5-0-5 | ME | the resnica axis |
| DOUBT / FAITH / HOPE / DESPAIR | 0-0-8 / 4-4-4 | E / JA | the dvom axis |
| DEATH / SLEEP / TRUTH / LOVE | 4-0-4 | JA | the metatime / love |
| VOICE: OD = silence | — | OD | the tišina axis |

## Key connections

- **The word has a body.** Every theme is a breath cycle + gesture + voice — the
  word-protocol is not just semantic (parcel-optikon) or vectorial
  (parcel-le-operator) but **embodied**. The beseda axis is a physical rhythm.
- **The four elements are rhythm signatures.** FIRE/WATER/AIR/EARTH each have a
  4-4-4 breath — the elements are states of the word's breath.
- **OD = silence is a voice.** Silence (tišina) is not the absence of voice but
  a *voice of its own* — OD. This confirms the tišina axis as an active register.

## Potentiality / Reliability / Repetition

- **Potentiality:** the **rhythm signature** gives the game a non-verbal prompt
  system — each theme/state has a breath+gesture+voice the player embodies.
  This is the word-protocol's physical layer.
- **Reliability:** MEDIUM — a poster/lexicon; the pattern (theme → rhythm) is
  consistent but the specific signs are personal.
- **Repetition:** the four elements, the resnica/dvom/tišina axes, and the
