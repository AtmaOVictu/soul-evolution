## Key connections

- **ogenj (fire) = Air's opposite** — its function is "necessity of change"
  (maps to ∆), its emotion is "power, destruction, transformation." This
  grounds Fire's treasure (revealed light) and its system (Illumination).
- **dih (breath) = the soul's motor** — "rhythm of living, fundamental
  impulse." This is Air's treasure (silence/breath) — the held breath.
- **Every word has a direction** — the word-protocol gives each soul a spatial
  tendency, not just a meaning. A soul carrying "resnica" is drawn
  above/right/through; one carrying "ne" is drawn left/middle/front.
