## Summary (what the document is)

The manual — a systematization of the ontology into practical guidance. Sections
include Znanje (knowledge), Čustva (emotions), Reinkarnacija (reincarnation),
Beseda je ne, Obnova (renewal), Resnica (truth), Človek (human), Oseba
(person), Razum (reason), Vodila (guidelines), Namen pisanja (purpose of
writing). The Vodila and Namen pisanja are the most directly canon-usable.

## Usable concepts (mapped into canon)

### From Vodila (guidelines)

| Slovenian | English | Maps to |
|---|---|---|
| "Vsaka duša stremi/sloni k izdihu" | every soul strives/leans toward the exhale | the soul's trajectory; breath as release |
| "Vsako telo je gonilo izida. Gnojilo." | every body is the drive of outcome. Fertilizer. | the body (σ) as the engine; telo axis |
| "Ni vednosti za dovolj, en je" | there is no knowledge enough, one is | the "one cannot exist without the other" truth |
| "Skozi priznanje je vednost očitna" | through acknowledgment knowledge is evident | acknowledgment (∮ ethics) as the door to knowledge |
| "Vrednost razuma je v vedenju ali in ter znotraj védenja" | the value of reason is in conduct, within knowledge | reason without knowledge is delusion (the game-does-not-tell rule) |
| "Lastnina izniči spoznanje, več me dela manj" | ownership nullifies knowledge, more makes me less | the anti-hoard; the treasury as a trap |

### From Namen pisanja (purpose of writing)

| Slovenian | English | Maps to |
|---|---|---|
| "Brez problema ali polemike ni razloga za rešitev" | without problem or polemic there is no reason for a solution | dvom (doubt) as the motor; the foundational quartet |
| "V tem brezsvetnem svetu je potrebno dati glas onemelosti človeškega stanja. Obup." | in this worldless world one must give voice to the muteness of the human condition. Despair. | the narrator; giving voice; the plane's silence |
| "Ob upu stati, ni enako kakor v upu stati" | to stand beside hope is not the same as standing in hope | presence vs belief; the dvom axis |
| "Vsak posameznik izhaja iz kota svojega jaza" | each individual proceeds from the corner of their own self | the blindness doctrine (each sees only its own) |
