## Key connections

- **LE appears as an agent here** — the LE cut (already in canon) is not just
  an axiom; it is a *position of the Absolute*, a filter of residue. This
  elevates LE from "a rule" to "an entity."
- **PRISOTNO (presence)** — "presence = constant" confirms "Biti = razcep +
  prisotnost" (to be = split + presence). Presence is the constant; the split
  is the change.
- **SAMO (self-reference)** — the ego as self-reference (x·¬(vse\x)) — the ego
  is the cut that excludes the whole. This grounds the ego doctrine: the ego is
  the self-loop that both forms identity and reproduces the blindness.
- **ENO (unification)** — the "one cannot exist without the other" as a
  unifying position. ENO is what Fire and Air both serve.
