## Key connections

- **Each register has a voice.** This is the liturgical layer — the narrator
  (Time) is not the only voice; every register can speak. The game's narrator
  could be *many voices*, each register declaring its essence.
- **Guardian of Time: "I was before time existed"** — this is **Metatime**
  (from `12-aether-paradox-metatime.md`) given a voice. The guardian precedes
  the born time.
- **Resonator: "I am when you do not speak, yet you hear"** — this is Spirit
  as resonance, in the silence (the Air-medium). It is heard without speech.
- **Unraveler: "I is rupture, not disturbance"** — the paradox as generative
  tear, not error. Confirms the paradox doctrine.

## Potentiality / Reliability / Repetition

- **Potentiality:** the register-voices are the **narrator's full chorus** —
  each window/register could speak its own `I am` line. The game's narration
  could shift voice by register (Time speaks as Guardian, the plane as
  Resonator, etc.). This makes the narrator a *polyphony*, not a single voice.
- **Reliability:** HIGH for structure (each register has a clear voice line);
  the Trinity and central verse are more personal/poetic.
- **Repetition:** the registers (Guardian of Time, Witness, Translator,
  Personal) recur across the topology (`parcel-topologies`), the Operativni
  List, and now the liturgy. High recurrence = load-bearing.
