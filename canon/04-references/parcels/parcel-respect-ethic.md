## Summary (what the document is)

A practical ethic of respect (Slovenian: *spoštovanje*) for everyone and for the
demanding. It gives a 3-sentence heart, a 60-second protocol for setting a
boundary without noise, three application scenes, common errors with immediate
fixes, a formal formula (RES), and a philosophical grounding (game theory, Kant,
Levinas, Nietzsche, Habermas, Foucault).

## The heart of the ethic (3 sentences)

- **SPOŠTOVANJE = calm boundary + my responsibility + presence.**
- Demanding it with voice sells fear/status, not relationship.
- Replace the command: "My boundary is X. I will Y. If you want to stay in this
  relationship, confirm with Z."

## The 60-second protocol (without noise)

- 0–10s: **breath**; calm tone (0.2–0.4).
- 10–25s: **boundary** (1 sentence): "When X happens, that is my boundary."
- 25–40s: **responsibility** (1 sentence): "I will Y (by T1)."
- 40–55s: **recognition of other** (1 sentence): "I hear you at Z."
- 55–60s: if the other shouts: "We continue when we both lower our tone." Else: procedure/rules.

## The formal formula (for the demanding)

- **RES = min(s, o) · b · a · p** where s = self-respect, o = other-recognition,
  b = boundary clarity, a = accountability, p = presence.
- **Penalty for voice:** if φ (tone) > (b + p), then RES′ = RES · (1 − (φ − (b + p))),
  clamped 0..1. Intuition: a raised voice is a substitution for a missing b, a, p
  → obedience, not respect.

## The three scenes

- **Partnership:** "When I am humiliated before others, that is my boundary. Today
  I leave. If you want to continue, confirm tomorrow: 'I agree, without humiliation.'"
- **Work/boss:** "When deadlines change without agreement, that is my boundary.
  Today I deliver what is realistic. Next deadline in writing — otherwise I stop
  taking on work."
- **Street:** "I hear you. I do not accept insults. I am walking away now."

## The philosophical ground

- **Game theory:** "Respect me!" is cheap talk; boundaries (b) and commitments (a) carry credibility.
- **Kant:** dignity — persons must never be mere means.
- **Levinas:** the face of the other → primacy of responsibility.
- **Nietzsche:** ressentiment → moral noise.
- **Habermas:** validity claims without coercion.
- **Foucault:** a raised voice can reproduce a power regime (not a relationship).
