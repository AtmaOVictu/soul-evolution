## Summary (what the document is)

A biography of Luka Pintarič (Slovenian musician-scholar, b. early 1990s,
Šentjur). Beyond the biography, it names the philosophy that grounds the entire
canon. The author is described as:
> "the logos and the anti-logos: the creative word and the destructive word

## The grounding philosophy (mapped into canon)

- **The Word is a primal "No."** The author redefines the Word as a primal No
  — "the void, the silence, the negation that paradoxically gives shape to
  meaning." This IS the canon's founding move: the Word (Beseda) and the No
  (Ne) are the same. **Beseda = Ne** — the word and the negation.
- **The "symbolic Satan"** — the questioning spirit that "drives the creation of
  new meaning." This is the opponent-agent (δ_D, el diablo) reframed as a
  creative principle — the dvom (doubt) as the engine of meaning.
- **The "living paradox" (Živi paradoks)** — "harmonizing the yes and the no."
  This is the Double Book's AX_MAX paradox made personal: the author lives both
  books simultaneously.
- **Corporeal existence enables the soul:** "breathing, having a body in time
  and space is what allows the emergence of something beyond the material — a
  soul or consciousness." This confirms the body (⊥) axis as the carrier of the
  soul.

## Key connections

- **The Word IS the No.** This is the single most important canon grounding —
  the Beseda (Word) and the Ne (No/negation) are the same. Every word the player
  speaks is simultaneously a negation (cut). This resolves the "Beseda Je Ne"
  title (parcel-beseda-je-ne) at the authorship level.
- **The opponent is the creative principle.** The "symbolic Satan" (questioning
  spirit) drives meaning. The dvom axis is not the enemy but the engine.
- **The living paradox is the author's whole stance.** The author lives both
  books (AX+MAX and PARADOX) — the player's ideal too.
