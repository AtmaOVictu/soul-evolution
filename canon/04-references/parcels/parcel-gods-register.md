A compendium (Books I·II·III) of gods from many pantheons (Zeus/Perun, Veles,
Thor, Loki, Hades, Osiris, Seth, Freya, Hermes, Svarog, Hel, Morana, Nüwa,

opponent, analog, hybrid, resonance).

## The usable structure (mapped into canon)

**The relation legend:**
| Relation | Maps to |
|---|---|
| P = star/child | the parent-child; the descent |
| M = partner | the ally; "one cannot exist without the other" |
| A = partner | the ally |
| X = opponent | the opposite; the war |
| = analog | the antinom twin |
| H = hybrid | the synthesis (Spirit) |
| resonance | the shared register |

**The fingerprints** — each god has a numeric value (Thor 5.0, Loki 6.0,
Hades 4.0, Osiris 7.0, Seth 8.5, Morana 5.0, ...). These are the archetypal
weights / potencies.

## Key connections

- **The gods are the personified registers.** The abstract registers (Guardian
  of Time, Witness, Translator) have mythic faces here — the gods are how the
  registers speak as characters. Seth (8.5) is the opponent; Morana (5.0) the
  winter/death; Thor (5.0) the guardian.
- **The relation legend is the canon's relation algebra.** P/M/A/X/=H/
  resonance cover every relation in the plane — but the game's core is the
  **X (opponent)** and the **resonance (Spirit)**. The gods embody these.
- **The fingerprints are the potencies.** Each archetype has a numeric weight —
  a value that could drive how that register behaves or how strongly it
  appears.

## Potentiality / Reliability / Repetition

- **Potentiality:** the gods are the **named archetypes** the player can
  encounter or embody — each with a fingerprint (potency) and relations. A
  god-register could seed the souls in the vault (each soul carries an
  archetypal fingerprint), or the black holes (events) could be god-events.
- **Reliability:** MEDIUM — the structure (relations + fingerprints) is clear,
  but the specific gods are many-pantheon and personal; not all are load-bearing
  for the game's core.
- **Repetition:** the opponent (X), the ally (M/A), the hybrid (Spirit), and
  the resonance recur across the canon. The relation legend is the coherent form
  of the plane's relational algebra.
