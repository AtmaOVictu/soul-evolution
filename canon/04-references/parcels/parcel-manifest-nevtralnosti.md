## Key connections

- **"speech is no"** — this is the ¬ operator as the fundamental act. The
  beseda axis is not about talking; it is about the "No" that cuts and thereby
  opens possibility (∫). This deepens the ¬ operator's role.
- **"every silence is an action"** — tišina is not empty; it is a choice. This
  connects to the treasuries: Air's silence is a *held* treasure, not an
  absence.
- **"neutrality is evil"** — this is the anti-doctrine to a "balanced" player.
  The alignment doctrine becomes sharper: not choosing is itself a choice, and
  in the moment of the war it is a detriment.
