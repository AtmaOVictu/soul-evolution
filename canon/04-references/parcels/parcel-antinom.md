## Summary (what the document is)

The Critical Retro Edition revisits each statement of "Word is Not" and gives
it an **antinom** (an antithesis). The whole becomes a **melody and
counter-melody at the same time** — "a map of presence and absence." Every
truth of the plane is paired with its own negation.

## The antinom structure (usable concept)

| Statement | Antinom |
|---|---|
| "Word is a necessary invention." | "Word is not a necessary invention — meaning can be without the mind." |
| "Word is Not — never Yes." | "Word is Yes — never Not." |
| "Word negates itself." | "Word confirms itself." |
| "Truth is a lie, into which I am ready to believe." | "Truth is not a lie — believing in it is not necessary." |
| "A human is part of everything. The peak of the world." | "A human is not the peak — it is his excessive outgrowth." |
| "God is only a word." | "God is more than a word." |
| "One A is impossible." | "One A is possible — absolute as a unit." |

## Key connections

- **The plane's truths are always paired.** Every canon truth (Word is Not,
  one cannot exist without the other, etc.) carries its antinom. This is the
  **paradox register** from `12-aether-paradox-metatime.md` — paradoxes are
  not errors, they are the *generative tears*. The antinom is the structural
  form of that tear.
- **"Truth is a lie, into which I am ready to believe"** — this is the
  resnica axis made honest: truth is a lie you choose to believe. This feeds
  the "game does not give knowledge lightly" and the blindness doctrine.
- **"Human is the peak of the world — or its excessive outgrowth"** — the
  split (razcep) as the defining condition: the peak or the outgrowth, both
  true.

## Potentiality / Reliability / Repetition

- **Potentiality:** the antinom could be a **game mechanic** — every revealed
  truth in the game is paired with its hidden antinom, which the player can
  find. The character sheet's opaque symbols (`Psi=0.xxx`) could each carry a
  hidden antinom (the "other truth"). This makes discovery playful and
  recursive.
- **Reliability:** HIGH — it is a complete, systematic edition (every
  statement antinom'd). It is a stable structural reference.
- **Repetition:** the antinom recurs across the canon — the split (razcep),
  the paradoxes, "one cannot exist without the other," the {A, ¬A} circle-bit.
  High recurrence = the antinom is a load-bearing form.
