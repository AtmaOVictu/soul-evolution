not need them"** is the source of the user's pointing: the foundational quartet
is **space, time (us), movement, and doubt** — and they are *wounded in doubt*.
This confirms `debate-log 015`: **doubt is the wound in the quartet; the
essential (bitna) one.**

It also adds a new note: **"death does not need them"** — death stands outside
the quartet. This gives the neutron/decay register a distinct source: death is
the thing that does not depend on space/time/movement/doubt.
