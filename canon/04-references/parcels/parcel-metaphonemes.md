## Key connections

- **The metaphonemes are the punctuation of silence.** Just as phonemes make the
  sounds of words, the metaphonemes make the *structure of silence* — the signs
  (silence, whisper, explosion, ellipsis, sinking, rising, silence-eye) that give
  the unspoken its legible form. This extends the word-protocol's audio layer
  into the visual-punctuation of silence.
- **Silence is the carrier, not the absence.** "Silence is absolute immanence —
  every sound is a derivation of silence." This confirms the Ontomandala Tišina
  (silence as foundation) and the Sound-Circle (silence as a sound).
- **The 𓂀 (silence-eye) is the witness.** The "all-seeing ∅nothing" — an absolute
  witness of silence — connects to the all-seeing/noise-as-witness themes and the
  omniscient-unreading of the canon's silent layer.
