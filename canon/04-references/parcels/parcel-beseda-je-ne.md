## Summary (what the document is)

A hybrid: **poetic diary + manifesto + dictionary of concepts.** Read in order
it is a flow; read by theme it is a tool. Core framing: "if you want to change
the world, you must know how to change the conscience (vest)." The "Ne" is the
primary cut (boundary), then a switch (je → ni, is → is-not), then a rhythm
(syllable/note/moment).

## The foundational quartet (Luka's pointing — canon emphasis)

> **prostor · čas · gibanje · dvom — in dvom je bitna/bistvena.**
> **(space · time · movement · doubt — and doubt is the essential one.)**

Within the plane, four registers are foundational: **space**, **time**,
**movement**, and **doubt**. Of these, **doubt is the essential (bitna) one**.
This is the canon's ordering of the axes: the quartet grounds the plane, and
doubt is its load-bearing member.

*(Refs: the axiom "samo v dvomu je gibanje mogoče" — only in doubt is movement
possible; the operator ¬ (negation/doubt); the dvom axis.)*
