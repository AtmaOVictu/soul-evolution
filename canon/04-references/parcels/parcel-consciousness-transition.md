## Summary (what the document is)

A mathematical model of consciousness transitions through phases (life, death,
consciousness) with circular dynamics in 3D space using the π function and
nonlinear transitions. The soul's movement is subject to emotional and social
influences that change its speed, direction, and intensity.

## The usable model

- **Four phases** of consciousness (life, death, consciousness, + one) in a
  multidimensional path.
- **Emotional/social influences:** **dvom (doubt), zaupanje (trust), iskrenost
  (sincerity), spoštovanje (respect)** — these change the speed, direction, and
  intensity of the transitions.
- **The method (from Teorije zarot):** "to formalize what usually remains
  unmeasurable — human consciousness, experience, inner processes — with the
  language of mathematics, logic, and ontology."
