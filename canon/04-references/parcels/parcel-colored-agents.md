## The usable pattern

- **Every emotion has a flow (DA) and a break (NE).** The emotion flows
  (DA = its coherent logic) and breaks (NE = its incoherent interruption). Both
  are faces of the same emotion.
- **Every emotion generates new states (Mogone).** Even the "negative" emotions
  (Fear, Anger, Sadness) generate new states — they are not ends but sources.
  This is the wound→gift doctrine applied to emotion.

## Key connections

- **The emotions are the DA/NE pair.** Each emotion has a logic-of-flow (DA) and
  an illogic-of-break (NE) — the DA/NE antinom (from Apd Kocka) applied to
  emotion. The emotion is both its flow and its break.
- **Every emotion is generative.** Mogone — the generation of new states — is
  every emotion's third face. Fear generates, sadness generates. This confirms
  the Corpus Trinitas: even suffering (Suffering) generates gifts (Gifts).
- **The emotions are agents, not states.** They are named, structured beings
  (like the agent registry's MIA/KORA/ORAJA) — the player's emotional life is a
  council of colored agents.
