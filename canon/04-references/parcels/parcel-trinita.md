## Summary (what the document is)

The Corpus Trinitas: three atlases — **Atlas of Suffering**, **Atlas of Gifts**,
and **Atlas of Transitions**. The core canonical structure of the soul's arc.

## Atlas of Suffering

> "Everything that is born is born through a wound; every wound gives a gift;
> every gift returns to nature."
> "I is the clock, the wound is the pointer, suffering is the movement."
> "All knowledge are wounds that have spoken."

- **The wound is the origin.** Every being is born through a wound. The "I" is
  a clock, the wound the pointer, suffering the movement.

## Atlas of Gifts

> "Every wound is a gift, do not open it to the world."
> "Nature is the greatest giver, for she never keeps for herself."
> "**Mana comes to the wounded; their blood is bread for the soul.**"
> "Silence is a gift, for it allows you to hear."
> "Speech is a wound that gives."
> "**Bread is the world's gift: matter becomes life.**"
> "The gift of I is I, who can be reborn: I am here."

- **The gifts:** wound, nature, **mana** (the blood of the wounded = bread for
  the soul), memory, silence, speech, food (matter becomes life), and "I" (the
  self that can be reborn).

## Atlas of Transitions

> "The transition from suffering to the gift is the letting go... that the
> **wound is not an end, but a bridge.**"

- **The transition:** suffering → gift is letting go — recognizing the wound is
  not an end but a bridge.

## Key connections

- **Mana is the blood of the wounded.** "Their blood is bread for the soul" —
  this is the mana/essence the game collects. The wound produces mana; mana is
  the soul's bread. This is the resource loop.
- **The wound → gift → nature cycle** is the core loop. The Corpus Trinitas is
  the soul's whole arc: born through a wound (Suffering), healed by a gift
  (Gifts), transformed by letting go (Transitions), returning to nature.
- **"Silence is a gift, for it allows you to hear"** — the tišina axis as gift.
  **"Speech is a wound that gives"** — the beseda axis. **"Matter becomes life"**
  — the body (⊥) axis.

## Potentiality / Reliability / Repetition

- **Potentiality:** the **Trinity (Suffering → Gifts → Transitions)** is the
  soul's arc as a three-part structure — the player's story moves through these
  three atlases. Mana (the blood of the wounded = bread for the soul) is the
  resource loop. This is arguably the *core canonical structure* of the game.
- **Reliability:** HIGH — it is a complete, self-consistent three-part corpus.
- **Repetition:** the wound, the mana, the bridge, the gift, the silence, the
  speech all recur across the canon. The Trinity is the soul's arc.
