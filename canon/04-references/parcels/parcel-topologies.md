## Key connections

- **The topology is the fabric's wiring.** The registers are not isolated; they
  connect in a definite flow. The crack topology runs GUARDIAN OF TIME →
  ... → PERSONAL_GATE — from Time to the individual soul. This is the
  generative chain: Time → the registers → the personal gate (the player).
- **The ring closes (∮).** The DNK spiral explicitly states the last pair
  returns to the first — the eternal cycle given as a topological fact. This is
  the mythos's cycle (war → grey → council → rebirth) made structural.
- **FORGIVENESS/MERCY/RELEASE** — the cycle has a *forgiving* register, not
  just destruction. This softens the eternal war: there is release.
