## Summary (what the document is)

An atlas of personal mandalas. Each mandala (Luka, LOVE, AGENTIC, SYMBOLIC,
COLLECTIVE, SPECIAL) is analyzed through two levels:
1. **Absolute** (logarithmic equation) — the mathematical notation of necessity
   that grows or declines;
2. **Immanence** — the embodiment of meaning in body, language, and agent.

## The two-level model (usable concept)

Every entity has:
- **Absolute** — its necessity as a logarithmic equation (log(1+|I|),
  log(t+1), log-spiral r=a·e^(bθ), lim log(x)/x=0).
- **Immanence** — how that necessity is embodied (a cut, a name, a filter, a
  root, a phase-agent, a transition).

The **positions** give direction: Above=birth/growth, Right=agentic,
Below=instinct/root, Left=mass/heart/memory, Above(again)=transcendence.

## Key connections

- **The two-level model is the canon method.** Every canon concept has an
  Absolute form (its equation/necessity) and an Immanent form (its embodiment).
  This is the parcel method made explicit — the canon reads a source, extracts
  the Absolute (the usable concept) and its Immanence (how it manifests).
- **The logarithms are growing/declining necessity.** log(1+|I|) grows with the
  self; −log(1−p) declines with warning. This is the "potentiality" frame — how
  a concept's necessity grows or decays.
- **LOVE = log(t+1), t=we** — love grows logarithmically with the "we" (the
  relation). This is the treasury/relation doctrine as a formula.
