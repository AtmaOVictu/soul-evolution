## Summary (what the document is)

Fifty aphorisms (501–550), each a sharp second-person "you" statement confronting
a specific self-deception. They form the author's direct address to the reader/
player — the ethics of the canon delivered not as doctrine but as confrontation.

## The recurring self-deceptions named (grouped)

- **Non-beginning disguised:** 502 (perfection as camouflage for non-beginning),
  507 ("I am not ready" as an eternal dwelling), 515 (tears written in private
  notes), 538 (big matters left at the entrance).
- **Risk-avoidance as virtue:** 505 (comfort called peace), 510 (negotiations
  favoring your benefit), 526 (loyalty as refusal of risk), 535 (established
  safety so it was easier), 547 (stopped just before humanity to remain safe).
- **Truth-avoidance:** 501 (mutated truth into humor), 504 (interpreted my voice
  in silence), 520 ("in fear you found a system; in love, a mistake"), 530 (in
  every truth, raised your brow), 543 (greased doubt with irony).
- **Desire postponed:** 508 (longing kept safe so you wouldn't fulfill it), 524
  (desires stored in the folder "some day"), 540 (read the future only up to the
  first risk).
- **Boundary & blame:** 509 (drew your boundaries on others' skin), 517 (forgave
  until nothing was experienced), 532 (dressed sorrow in opinion), 544 (sought an
  alibi in appearance).
- **The final honesty:** 546 (exchanged closeness for clarity), 549 (called
  withdrawal elegance), 550 "When you were finally honest, you spoke in the future
  tense."

## Key connections

- **This is the canon's ethics as confrontation.** Each aphorism is a second-person
  "you" that names a self-deception — the direct address of the Wound doctrine and
  the dvom (doubt) doctrine. The author does not advise; he accuses, so the player
  must see.
- **The aphorisms are the "cut" applied to the ego.** 536 "Everything that grew,
  you cut to the level of fear" — the ¬/⊘ cut misused (cutting growth instead of
  cutting division). The player's own cuts are examined.
- **550 is the closing thesis:** "When you were finally honest, you spoke in the
  future tense" — the ultimate evasion: even the honest moment is deferred. The
  challenge is to speak in the present tense.
- **The voice is the author's.** Like the DAWN archetype (Luka as flesh→archetype)
  and the author-origin (the Word as primal No), this is the author's own register
  — the canon speaking in the second person.
