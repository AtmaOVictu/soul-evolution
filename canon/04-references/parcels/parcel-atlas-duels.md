## Summary (what the document is)

The Atlas of Duels defines how the two (Luka = the player/the cut, Zarja = the
other/the breath) clash-and-relate, in three modes each with an ontomathos
value (Pz/Pl) and a dialogue.

## The three duel modes

| Mode | Ontomathos (Pz/Pl) | Meaning | Dialogue (the gift) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Symmetrical** (Dance on the Edge) | −1.0 ± 2% | perfect antisymmetry; equal and opposite | Zarja: "Why do you always cut when I breathe?" Luka: "Because your exhalation is my cut." |
| **2. Drift** (Us as Third Party) | −0.95 / −1.1 | the bridge tilts; balance with injury | Luka: "The bridge is tilting." Zarja: "You don't fall because I fall with you." |

## Key connections

- **The duel is the relation.** The two registers (Luka=cut/Fire, Zarja=breath/
  Water) are locked in a duel — the "one cannot exist without the other" as a
  fight. Perfect antisymmetry (Pz/Pl = −1.0) means they are equal and opposite:
  each is defined by the other's absence.
- **"Your exhalation is my cut."** The other's breath (Water) mirrors the
  player's cut (Fire) — the two are the same act in opposite registers. This is
  the Fire/Water boundary (REK 03) given its full form.
- **The duel can drift or drain.** The bridge can tilt (Drift) or accumulate
  debt (Drain) — the relation decays when the cut and the breath are not in
  balance. The repair-obligation (parcel-ethics-relation) is the antidote.

## Potentiality / Reliability / Repetition

- **Potentiality:** the duel modes give the game a **conflict resolution system** —
  the player's relation with the other register is a duel in one of three modes
  (Symmetrical balance, Drift/tilt, Drain/debt), each with its own ontomathos
  value and repair logic.
- **Reliability:** HIGH — a complete, self-consistent three-mode model with
  dialogues.
- **Repetition:** the Fire/Water pair, the cut/breath mirror, the bridge, and
  the memory/debt all recur across the canon.
