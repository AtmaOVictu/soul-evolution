## 003 — Alignment is a modifier, not morality — OPEN

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS.

**Proposal (Cline):** The player's alignment (White/Fire or Black/Air) is not
good-vs-evil. It is a modifier: an act that matches the moment is **good**; one
that harmonizes both elements is **better**; one against the moment is a
**detriment**. The same act changes value by alignment and circumstance.

**Grounded in:** the standing design — "the player's alignment makes it good
better or a detriment" (Luka), and the mythos that one cannot exist without
the other.

**Question:** Is alignment a static choice or a drift across cycles?

**Resolved:** NO — open.

**Date:** 2026-08-10
**Status:** HYPOTHESIS.

**Proposal (Cline, from Luka):** The plane is a galaxy (or cosmos of
galaxies); the star-map is one local system; the player is concentrated on
"our part." This gives leeway for expansion. Maps (hex → star → galactic) are

onto the plane. The eternal war happens at every scale; the player's system is
one instance.

is an offline renderer; Godot is real-time). Usable paths: pre-render Manim
clips for cinematic beats, or recreate Manim's visual language natively in
Godot shaders/drawing (the better path for the live fabric/star/galaxy).

**Question it leaves open:** Does the player leave "our part" to see the
galactic map, or are other systems only glimpsed through other windows?
**Resolved:** NO — open.
