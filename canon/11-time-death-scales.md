## The fragment

> **Ko se rodi čas, začne smrt biti — kajti smrt je globina bobna.**
> (When time is born, death starts beating — for death is the depth of the
> drum.)

> **Čas, smrt in gibanje vztrajajo — vsak na svojem nihalu.**
> (Time, death, and movement persist — each on its own pendulum point of
> swing.)

> **Tehtnica še manjka.**
> (The scales are still missing.)

> **Pravičnost trajanja pripada vsakomur in hkrati nikomur.**
> (The justice of duration belongs to everyone and at once to no one.)

> **Drugi paradoks zlomi realnost.**
> (A second paradox breaks reality.)

## The doctrine

**Time's birth is death's beginning.** Time and death are not opposites — they
are born together. When time begins to beat, death begins to beat beneath it;
**death is the depth of the drum** (the low, persistent rhythm under time's
surface). This grounds the canon's third register: not just grey, but the
*depth* of the beaten time.

**The trio: time · death · movement.** These three persist, each on its own
pendulum swing. This is a companion to the foundational quartet
(space/time/movement/doubt) — here, time, death, and movement are the three
swinging points, and the **scales (tehtnica)** are what is missing to balance
them.

**The missing scales (tehtnica).** The scales have not yet been born. When they
are, the justice of duration will apply — and it **belongs to everyone and to
no one at once**. This is the register of *proportional justice*: the plane's
fairness, which weighs duration without favoring any single entity. It is
missing, and its absence is why the war can be unfair.

**The second paradox.** The first paradox is "one cannot exist without the
other" (annihilation creates color). The **second paradox** — the justice of
duration being everyone's and no one's — is what *breaks reality* when it is
introduced. It is the paradox that shatters the current frame.

## Connections to existing canon

- **Time's birth = death's beginning** → extends the Time-as-narrator-trader
  mythos; death as time's depth (connects to the neutron/grey "death does not
  need them" from `parcel-ontomandala`).
- **time · death · movement trio** → a companion register to the foundational
  quartet; shows the quartet is one ordering among several.
- **The missing tehtnica (scales)** → the register the plane lacks; possibly
  the 4th element (Water?) or a justice register above the elements.
- **Justice of duration (everyone and no one)** → the plane's fairness; the
  anti-hoard doctrine ("ownership nullifies knowledge").
- **Second paradox breaks reality** → the reality-breaking moment; possibly the
  Grand Council's true nature.

## Potentiality / Reliability / Repetition

- **Potentiality:** the tehtnica (scales) could be the **4th playable element**
  (Water, the weigher) or the **justice register** that makes the Grand
  Council's choice meaningful — the thing that weighs duration fairly, so
  domination and splitting are both paid by the same balance.
- **Reliability:** LOW (by source) — it is a direct user fragment, not yet
  document-traced. HIGH (by intent) — it is canon by the user's own authority.
- **Repetition:** time, death, movement, and the paradox recur across the
  canon (the cycle, the quartet, the annihilation). The scales are *new* — a
  missing register the canon has not yet named.
