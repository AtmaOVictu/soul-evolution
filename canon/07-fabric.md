# 07 — The Quantum Fabric (strings)

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — v0.01. The plane is not only elements and
> entities — it is woven from an underlying **quantum fabric / strings**. This
> is the substrate the elements are patterns of, the medium the souls vibrate
> on, and the face the liquid hex-grid already shows.

## The principle

Everything in the plane — Fire, Air, the souls, the neutrons, dust, even the
treasure that flows — is a **pattern in the fabric**. The fabric is the
substrate; the entities are the knots and resonances upon it. The visible
lattice of this fabric is the **hex grid**; its living vibration is the
**liquid wave** that already ripples as `world_color` rises.

*(ref: `scripts/hex_grid.gd` — the wave/ripple modulation; `scripts/main.gd` —
the `world_color` bleed toward violet.)*

## Strings as the source of the souls

In the physics the mythos borrows, fundamental particles are **different
vibration modes of the same strings**. Here:

- **White (Fire)** = one vibration mode of the fabric.
- **Black (Air)** = another, opposite mode.
- **Neutron** = an inert, quiet mode — the fabric at rest, unstrung.
- The souls are **gatherings** in the fabric — knots where the strings
  resonate.

## The operators as threads

The operators are the **threads** of the fabric:

| Operator | The thread |
|---|---|
| **∇** (divergence) | a thread spreading outward |
| **¬** (negation) | a thread cut / cut against |
| **∆** (change) | a thread shifting position |
| **✡** (trace) | a thread leaving a light-mark |
| **τ** (time) | the thread's own extension |
| **σ** (body) | a knot of threads gathered |

*(ref: `04-references/operators.md`.)*

## Spirit as resonance

When Fire and Air vibrate against each other in the fabric, they do not only
annihilate — they **resonate**. That resonance is **Spirit**: the fabric's own
response to the two modes meeting. Spirit is always present because the fabric
is always under the two vibrations; it is the chord between them.

## Time moves along the fabric

Time is not a thread but the **extension of the fabric** — the dimension along
which everything travels. Time is the narrator (it reads the fabric's history)
and the trader (it prices how much of the fabric's extension you spend). This
is why Time's treasure is **duration**: the fabric's length is what it holds.

## The blindness as a fabric condition

Monochrome is the fabric when neither mode can perceive the other's pattern.
Color is the fabric's resonance becoming visible. The blindness ends when Time
facilitates the perception — when the two modes are made to resonate together.
