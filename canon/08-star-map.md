# 08 — The Star Map (Fire as a directional-light system)

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — v0.01. The Fire system (Illumination) is not a
> uniform glow — it is a **map system from above**, lit by a star, with light
> coming from directions, and entities that amplify or diminish it.

## The principle

The plane is a **moving system around a star**. Light does not come from
everywhere evenly — it arrives **from directions** as the system moves. This is
the Fire system's true form: a tactical map from above where light is
directional, and every entity in space either **amplifies** or **diminishes**
the light passing through it.

This is the "aftershock" of the origin: the light that reaches the plane is a
**resonance of how it was before Time** — before Time traded the sand and
breathed time into clay.

## The mechanic

**Directional light on a top-down map.** The star is a moving source; its light
sweeps the plane from a direction. Each entity in space affects that light:

- **Amplifiers** — entities that brighten what passes (Fire-aligned, the
  white souls, revealed light).
- **Diminishers** — entities that cast shadow / eat the light (Air-aligned,
  the dark souls, concealment).

The player reads the map by light: what is lit reveals; what is shadowed hides.
This makes the Illumination system a **positional, directional** mechanic — not
just "fire a beam" but "where the star's light falls, and who bends it."

**The aftershock quality:** the light is not steady — it undulates like a
resonance, the leftover vibration of the pre-Time state. The liquid wave of the
hex grid is the visible form of this aftershock.

## Tie to the origin

Before Time, light was pure aftershock — directionless, repeating. Time's
first act was to **trade the sand** (the dust resource) and **breathe time
into clay**, giving light a direction and the plane a ground. The star is the
anchor Time gave to the aftershock; the moving system is the breathing of that
first breath.

*(ref: the origin myth — "aftershocks of how it was before time used the sand
traded and given to breathe time into clay.")*

## The scene it feeds

A scene where the player stands on the top-down map and watches the star's
light sweep, reading the plane by what is lit and what is shadowed — the first
taste of directional, positional light as a tactical instrument.
