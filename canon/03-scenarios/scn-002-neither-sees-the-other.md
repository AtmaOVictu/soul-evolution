# Scenario 002 — Neither Sees the Other

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** resnica (truth) × tišina (silence)
> **Reference:** `04-references/operators.md` ("Zavest se rodi, ko prenehamo
> verjeti v vprašanje kot gotovost" — consciousness is born when we stop
> believing the question is certainty); `04-references/txt.md` (axiom — "space
> is the condition of exchange; time is the condition of change").

## The image

A white soul and a black soul in the same room, each unable to see the other's
value. The white sees only its own light; the black only its own dark. They are
side by side and utterly alone — this is the monochrome.

## The mechanic

**Monochrome-as-blindness.** Until color/substance emerges, a white soul
literally cannot perceive a black soul beyond concealment distance, and vice
versa. The player experiences the mutual blindness directly: the enemy is
invisible until Time facilitates the perception (color appears).

**Rule being introduced:** the blindness is the grey. Color is the *perception*
of the other, not an absence being filled.

## The scene it feeds

A tension scene where the player's soul is near an unseen enemy. The narrator
(Time) observes: *"light notices only what is light's."* The player must choose
whether to reveal color (spend duration) to see the enemy — or to remain blind
and safe.

## Canon claim

Instantiates the blindness doctrine in the mythos (`00-mythos.md`): the plane
is grey not because color is absent, but because neither side can perceive the
other.
