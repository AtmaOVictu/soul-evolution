# Scenario 009 — The Exchange of Treasures

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** beseda (the word) × čas (time)
> **Reference:** `05-treasures.md` (the designated currencies); the mythos
> doctrine that Time is the trader; `04-references/txt.md` ("The only true
> value is time").

## The image

A white soul holds its revealed light — a bright moment, a truth made visible.
A black soul holds its silence — a held breath, a hollow it has treasured.
They cannot spend each other's treasure; they cannot even see it. But Time
stands between them, naming both, and offers the exchange: *give what you keep,
receive what you cannot see.*

## The mechanic

**Trade between designated currencies, brokered by Time.** Each entity has a
treasure it can perceive and spend (its own). Time is the only entity that can
handle all currencies. The player, by alignment, may:

- **offer their own treasure** (revealed light if White, silence if Black) to
  Time,
- **receive the other's treasure** in exchange — the price of perception.

This is the concrete mechanism of the blindness ending: to see the other's
value, you must offer your own. The trade is the bridge the mythos describes.

**The windows are the treasure-hoards.** What the player offers and receives is
displayed as art in windows — to be hoarded, upgraded, traded.

## The scene it feeds

A scene where the player meets the opposite polarity's treasure for the first
time and must decide whether to trade their own for the power to perceive it.
The narrator (Time) states the terms plainly: *"to see, you must spend what you
keep."*

## Canon claim

Instantiates the treasury doctrine (`05-treasures.md`) and the Time-as-trader
mythos. Trade between currencies is a **new system** — the economy of
designated treasures — not a repurposed point pool.
