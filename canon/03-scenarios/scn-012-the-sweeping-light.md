# Scenario 012 — The Sweeping Light

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** prostor (space) × čas (time)
> **Reference:** `08-star-map.md` (the star map); `06-systems.md` (Fire system).

## The image

From above, the plane is a moving system around a star. Light is not a uniform
glow — it sweeps in from a direction as the system turns. The player stands on
the map and reads it by light: what is lit is revealed, what is shadowed is
hidden. And every entity in space bends that light — some amplify it, some
diminish it.

## The mechanic

**Directional light on a top-down tactical map.** The star's light arrives from
a direction and sweeps as the system moves. Entities **amplify** (Fire-aligned)
or **diminish** (Air-aligned) the light they receive. The player reads the
battlefield by the light field: position yourself in the lit to be seen, in the
shadow to be hidden — or be the one bending the light.

This is the Fire (Illumination) system as a **positional, directional
mechanic**, not just a firing beam.

## The scene it feeds

A scene where the player must use the sweeping light to see the enemy — or
hide — understanding that light is directional and the plane moves under it.

## Canon claim

Instantiates the star map (`08-star-map.md`). The Fire system is a light-field
tactical map, not a beam. The light is an aftershock — directional, moving.
