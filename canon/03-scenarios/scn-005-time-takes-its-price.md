## The image

There is a voice that does not fight. It stands between Fire and Air and names
what they do to each other. And it does not give — it **trades**. For every
color it reveals, it takes a measure of the age itself. The faster color flows,
the sooner the war ends.

## The mechanic

**Time as trader — it takes duration.** Every annihilation does two things at
once:
- reveals **color** (raises `world_color`),
- advances **death** (raises `death_count`, driving the neutron-weight toward
  the Grand Council).

So revealing color is not a free gift — it **accelerates the end of the current
age**. The player who grinds for color is spending the war's own duration to
get it. This makes "grinding for color" a real tension: more color now, but a
shorter Eternal War before the next lock.

## The scene it feeds

A scene where the player must choose: reveal color quickly (and move faster
toward the Council) or hoard the grey (and stay in the war longer, but blind).
The narrator (Time) states the price plainly, without judgment.

## Canon claim

Instantiates Time as trader (`00-mythos.md`, `CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §8). Time is
the **narrator** (the voice) and the **trader** (it prices duration). The
economy of perception is: color in exchange for the age's remaining time.
