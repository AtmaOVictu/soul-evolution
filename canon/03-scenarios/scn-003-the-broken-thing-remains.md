# Scenario 003 — The Broken Thing Remains

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** telo (body) × prostor (space)
> **Reference:** `04-references/operators.md` ("Biti = razcep + prisotnost" —
> to be = split + presence); `CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §8 (neutron-remnant).

## The image

When Fire and Air meet, they do not vanish into nothing. What is left is a
**broken form** — a shard that was once a sphere but lost its roundness. It is
neither fire nor air; it is the grey residue of the meeting. It stays where it
fell, and the plane grows heavy with such wreckage.

## The mechanic

**Annihilation → color + neutron.** When two souls (opposite polarity) make
contact, they produce:
- **color** (substance, world-level) — the plane gains hue,
- **neutron** (a broken-object remnant, physical) — a shard obstacle left at
  the contact point.

The neutron is **not** a generic obstacle. It is a *corpse-shard* — echoing the
broken look, but inert and grey. It blocks movement. That is what makes
"movement grows heavy" literal: the arena fills with the bodies of its own war.

**The neutron can be reclaimed.** A later annihilation near enough to a
neutron-remnant cracks it back open — destroying the remnant and feeding
`world_color`. Old wreckage is not permanently spent; new conflict can free it.

## The scene it feeds

After the first annihilation, the player sees the shard remain. The narrator
(Time) names it: *"the broken thing remains. this is what the meeting leaves."*
The player learns that violence leaves a body — and that the body can be freed
by further violence.

## Canon claim

Instantiates the three-state physics (`02` in the mythos) and the neutron
resolution (`CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §8). The neutron is a **new system** (a
corpse-shard object), not a repurposed obstacle.
