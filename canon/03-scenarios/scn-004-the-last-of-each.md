## The image

The plane has gone heavy. The wreckage of the war has locked all movement. Only
two remain — the last member of Fire and the last member of Air. They have
fought for eternity and now they must meet, not to fight, but because the
weight has made fighting impossible.

## The mechanic

**The Grand Council.** When the accumulated neutron-weight crosses a threshold,
movement locks. The last surviving member of each side is forced to a meeting.
Two choices are offered:

- **Domination** — one color takes all. But the plane does not allow a single
  color to persist: it forcibly reconverts ~30% of the "winning" color back to
  the opposite polarity, because annihilation (the source of color) requires
  *two* colors. Domination is the vain choice, paid after the fact, at a worse
  ratio.
- **Splitting** — the resources are divided up front, evenly, voluntarily. It
  pays the same tax but efficiently and without the post-choice penalty.

Either way, the truth is enforced as a rule, not narration: **one cannot exist
without the other.**

## The scene it feeds

A quiet, heavy scene — the two survivors face each other as the narrator
(Time) lays out the choice. The player decides: expensive domination or
efficient splitting. Both lead to the Rebirth.

## Canon claim

Instantiates the Grand Council (`03` in the mythos) and the domination/splitting
resolution (`CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §8). The council is a **new system** — a
negotiation state, not a combat state.
