# Scenario 010 — The Hand That Works the System

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** um (the mind) × prostor (space)
> **Reference:** `06-systems.md` (the entity-interactive systems); the mythos
> doctrine that the player constructs reality by acting through the systems.

## The image

The player's hand is not outside the plane — it is *in* it, working the
systems. To fire is to work Fire's system. To hide is to work Air's. To break
the wreckage is to work Neutron's. Each act is an engagement with an entity's
machine, and the machine answers.

## The mechanic

**The player interacts with each entity's system directly.** The six systems
(Illumination, Concealment/Repulsion, Wreckage, Building, Trade/Narration,
Harmony) are not abstract — they are the player's own verbs. Every action the
player takes in a scene is an interaction with one of them.

The rule from `06-systems.md`: each entity owns its system. The player does not
observe the mythos from outside; they *participate* by working the systems. The
narrator (Time) names each act as it lands.

## The scene it feeds

A scene that introduces the player's first system interaction — the feeling
that their actions are not "moving a token" but *operating the plane's
machinery*. The narrator: *"you are not outside. you are the hand at the
wheel."*

## Canon claim

Instantiates the systems doctrine (`06-systems.md`). The player's agency is
expressed as interaction with entity-systems, not as abstract input. This is
the concrete form of "simulating reality by constructing narratives" — you
build the reality by working its systems.
