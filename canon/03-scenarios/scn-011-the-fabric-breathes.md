# Scenario 011 — The Fabric Breathes

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** prostor (space) × telo (body)
> **Reference:** `07-fabric.md` (the quantum fabric); `scripts/hex_grid.gd`
> (the liquid wave); `scripts/main.gd` (the `world_color` violet bleed).

## The image

Look closely and the plane is not still. It is a **fabric** that breathes — a
substrate of strings that ripples, vibrates, and bleeds toward violet as the
war produces color. The hex grid is not decoration; it is the fabric's visible
lattice. The souls are not tokens on a board; they are **gatherings** in this
fabric, knots where the strings resonate.

## The mechanic

**The fabric is the substrate.** The player feels the plane as a living medium:
- the **wave** (`hex_grid.gd`) is the fabric's ongoing vibration,
- the **violet bleed** (`world_color`) is the fabric's resonance becoming
  visible as color accumulates,
- the souls are **vibration modes** — White one mode, Black another, Neutron an
  inert quiet one.

The player's actions resonate in the fabric. To fire is to excite its strings;
to annihilate is to strike a chord that rings color into the world.

## The scene it feeds

A scene of quiet between battles where the player, standing still, becomes
aware of the fabric breathing beneath them — the plane as a live medium, not a
static board. The narrator (Time) allows a rare moment of silence: *"you are
not standing on the plane. you are standing in it."*

## Canon claim

Instantiates the quantum fabric (`07-fabric.md`). The plane is a substrate, not
a container; the hex grid and wave are its face. This is the ground the other
systems (elements, entities, treasures) are woven into.
