# Scenario 014 — The Many Windows of the Plane

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.

## The image

The plane is a galaxy, and the player is concentrated on one small part of it.
But they are not trapped there — they see the whole through **windows**. Each
window is a perspective: one shows the local star-map, one shows the galactic
sweep, one shows an entity's treasure, one shows a moment of the eternal war.
Perspective is everything; the game is a set of windows onto the plane.

## The mechanic

**Maps are integral; perspective is all.** The player reads the plane through
maps at different scales — hex tile, star-map, galactic map — and through
windows that are perspectives. The galaxy gives **leeway**: the local war is
one instance of a war that happens everywhere, and the player can shift
perspective to see more, or concentrate on their part.

The player is not bound to the local system; they can widen the window to see
the galactic truth, or close it to act in their corner. The maps and windows
are the interface of the plane.

## The scene it feeds

A scene where the player pulls back from the local star-map to see the galaxy —
many stars, many wars, all instances of the same conflict — and understands
that their war is one breath of a larger breathing. The narrator (Time):
*"yours is not the only fire. it is only the one you are in."*

## Canon claim

Instantiates the galactic scale and the perspective/window doctrine
(`10-galaxy.md`). Maps (hex → star → galaxy) are the integral interface; the
windows are the perspectives onto the plane.
