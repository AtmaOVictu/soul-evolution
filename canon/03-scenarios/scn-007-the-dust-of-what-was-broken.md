# Scenario 007 — The Dust of What Was Broken

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** pot (path) × čas (time)
> **Reference:** `CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §9 (the resource economy).

## The image

The broken shards of the war can be broken further. Grind a neutron-remnant
long enough and it does not vanish — it becomes **dust**: formless, grey,
common. The dust of what was already broken. It is the lowest tier of the
plane's remains, and it is everywhere.

## The mechanic

**Dust is the common currency of the character.** The chain:
- matter + antimatter → **color** (rare, world-level) + **neutron** (broken
  object, physical).
- neutron + more violence → **dust** (common, formless, character-level).

Dust feeds the **character-builder** — the economy by which a player shapes
their own soul (abilities, upgrades). It is the everyday currency; color is the
rare world-changer; feats are the rarest, cycle-level permanence. Different
scarcity, different scope, same act of violence.

**The game does not give knowledge lightly.** No UI popup explains "use dust to
upgrade." The player discovers the economy by playing, not by being told.

## The scene it feeds

A scene where the player grinds a neutron-remnant into dust and must decide
what to build with it — without any tooltip telling them what the dust is for.

## Canon claim

Instantiates the dust resource (`CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §9). Dust is a **new
system** — a third resource tier, not a repurposed point pool. Character
building is integral, made meaningful by dust-scarcity.
