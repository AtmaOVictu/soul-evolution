# Scenario 006 — What the Feat Costs

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** značaj (character) × resnica (truth)
> **Reference:** `CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §8 (feats as infinite escalation); the
> mythos blindness doctrine (`00-mythos.md`).

## The image

A side that has survived many cycles carries its scars as **feats** — permanent
marks of power. But each feat is not free. As the side grows strong, it grows
blind: it sees less of the other side coming. Power feeds on perception.

## The mechanic

**Feats widen the blindness.** Each cycle's rebirth grants a feat (permanent
power). But each feat also widens that side's blindness radius — the
concealment mechanic (`_update_concealment` in `main.gd`) scaling the reveal
radius down as accumulated feats rise. A heavily-feated side is stronger and,
mechanically, worse at seeing the enemy approach.

**The paradox:** power without perception reproduces the original blindness
that started the war. A feat eventually becomes its own kind of grey. There is
no hard cap — the cycle is eternal — but escalation devours the danger-sense.

## The scene it feeds

A scene where a powerful, heavily-feated soul is ambushed by something it
cannot see — because its own power has blinded it. The narrator: *"strength is
not sight."*

## Canon claim

Instantiates the feat-escalation resolution (`CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §8): no cap,
but power couples to a perception cost. This is a **new system** (blindness
tied to accumulated feats), not a hard-ceiling patch.
