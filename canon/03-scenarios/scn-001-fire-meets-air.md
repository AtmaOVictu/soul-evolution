## The image

A wall of fire and a field of air. Between them, the first neutral ground — a
place neither element can hold alone. Where they meet, a thin grey line
appears: the beginning of the plane.

## The mechanic

The first contact between a white soul (Fire) and a black soul (Air). On
contact, they do not simply destroy each other — they **annihilate into
Spectrum** (color) and **leave a neutron** (broken grey). The player sees, for
the first time, that the conflict *produces* the plane's substance.

**Rule being introduced:** matter + antimatter → color + neutron. The player
learns the three-state physics by watching the first pair.

## The scene it feeds

The opening scene. The player chooses alignment (White or Black), and the first
soul they control meets the opposite polarity. The narrator (Time) names what
happens: *"fire meets air. the plane begins."*

## Canon claim

This scenario instantiates **debate-log 001** (Fire/Air) and **002** (Spirit
always present): the meeting is not a loss but a first production.
