# Scenario 013 — Earth Against the Engulfing

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — seed building-block.
> **Axis:** značaj (character) × pot (the path)
> **Reference:** `09-earth.md` (Earth, the third playable element); the origin
> myth (Time breathed time into clay).

## The image

The player is Earth — the clay Time gave life. Around it, the forces press in:
Fire seeks to consume and lift, Air seeks to erode and scatter. Earth does not
move to meet them. Earth holds. Earth is the ground the engulfing forces break
against.

## The mechanic

**Play as Earth — resistance and steadiness.** The third scenario is defensive:
the player, aligned as Earth, holds ground while Fire and Air surround them.
Earth's system is **Steadiness / Grounding** — it holds, resists, and roots the
sweeping light. It does not illuminate (Fire) nor conceal (Air); it **endures**.

The cost of Earth is that it is always engulfed — the forces are always
pressing. The reward is that it is never destroyed; it is the stable thing the
war happens over.

## The scene it feeds

A scene where the player, as Earth, withstands the closing forces — surviving
the engulf, holding the ground, becoming aware that the war is fought *over*
them, not *in* them.

## Canon claim

Instantiates Earth as the third playable element (`09-earth.md`). Earth's
scenario is resistance against engulfment — a distinct register from Fire's
illumination and Air's concealment.
