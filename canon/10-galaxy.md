# 10 — The Galaxy (scale and perspective)

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW** — v0.01. The plane is not a single system — it is
> **galactic**. We are concentrated on our part of it. This gives leeway: the
> war is everywhere, but the player sees their corner. Maps are integral;
> perspective is all — the **windows** are the perspectives.

## The principle

The plane is a **galaxy** (and beyond, a cosmos of galaxies). The star-map
(`08-star-map.md`) is one **local system** within it. The player is concentrated
on **our part** — the local system, the wars that happen here. But the galaxy is
vast, and that vastness is **leeway**: room to expand, other systems, other
stars, other local wars, all instances of the same eternal Fire/Air conflict.

At every scale the same truth holds: Fire and Air fight, Time trades, Spirit is
made, the plane greys and is reclaimed. The player's system is one breath of a
much larger breathing.

*(ref: `08-star-map.md` — the local star; extends to the galactic.)*

## Maps are integral

The interface of the plane is **maps** — from the single hex tile, to the
local star-map, to the galactic map of many systems. Each is a register of the
same war:

| Map | Scale | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **Hex grid** | tile | the microstructure, the fabric |
| **Star-map** | local system | one star, directional light, the local war |
| **Galactic map** | many systems | the plane's breadth, where the war happens everywhere |

The maps are not decoration — they are the **perspective** by which the player
reads the plane.

## Perspective is all — the windows are perspectives

The **windows** (the frames that feature art) are not just treasure-hoards —
they are **perspectives**. Each window is a way of seeing the plane: a map, a
scene, an entity's treasure, a moment of the mythos. Perspective is everything;
the game is a set of windows onto the plane.

The player's perspective is chosen and shifted — they see the galaxy through
windows, and each window is a truth about the local war.

## The leeway

Because the plane is galactic and the player is concentrated on one part, there
is **leeway** for more: other systems to journey to, other local conflicts, other
elements (Earth is one; Water may come), other scales. The canon is not closed —
the galaxy is the room it grows into.
