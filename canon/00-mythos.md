## The primeval division

Before the plane, there was **Light** and **Dark**, and they fought for space.
Neither could hold the whole; space had to be divided. So the **Grand
Compromise** was struck:

- **Light gained FIRE.** *(ref: `04-references/eng-shit.md` — "As LIGHT — I
  shine in the cracks, not to heal, but to reveal, to slice through the dark.")*
- **Dark gained AIR.** *(ref: `04-references/zlo.md` — "evil doesn't originate
  from him directly, but arises in his silence" — the air is the medium of
  absence.)*

Fire and Air are the two faces of the plane's substance. Neither alone is
whole; neither can exist without the other.

## Spirit

**Both Light and Dark make SPIRIT, and Spirit is always present.**

Spirit is the *third* thing that neither produces alone — what arises in the
space between Fire and Air when they act upon each other. This is the
load-bearing wall: the game's "one cannot exist without the other" is not
narration, it is the physics of Spirit.

*(ref: `04-references/operators.md` — the LE axiom: "the word is not a means
but an ontological cut"; Spirit is the LE cut between Fire and Air.)*

## Time — the narrator and the trader

Time facilitated the fight. Time did not take a side. Time is:

- **The narrator** — the voice that names what Fire and Air do to each other.
- **The trader** — the hand that exchanges value between them.

Time never creates; it reveals and it prices. What it reveals is **color
(substance)**. What it prices is **duration** — the faster you reveal color,
the faster the current age ends. *(ref: `CRITICAL_BRIEF.md` §8, "Time as
trader — it takes duration.")*

## The blindness

**Light notices only what is Light's. Dark notices only what is Dark's.**

Each side is structurally blind to the other's value. This blindness *is* the
monochrome: the plane is grey not because color is absent, but because neither
side can perceive what belongs to the other. Color appears when Time
facilitates perception — when Light finally sees Dark's value, and vice versa.

## The eternal cycle

The cosmos breathes in four acts:

1. **The Eternal War** — Fire and Air clash; annihilation produces **color**
   (substance) and **neutrons** (the broken, neutral residue).
2. **The Greying** — neutrons accumulate; the plane becomes heavy with its own
   wreckage. Movement slows.
3. **The Grand Council** — the accumulated weight locks motion. The last of
   each side are forced to meet. They choose — domination or splitting — and
   both fail against the truth: one cannot exist without the other.
4. **The Rebirth** — color reappears on the grand map. Each side gains a
   **feat**. The fight amplifies. The grind continues.

## Alignment

The player chooses an alignment (White/Light/Fire or Black/Dark/Air). Alignment
is **not** good-vs-evil. It is a modifier: an act aligned with the moment is
**good**, an act that harmonizes both is **better**, an act against the moment
is a **detriment**. The same act changes value by alignment and circumstance.

*(ref: `04-references/txt.md` axiom — "space is the condition of exchange;
time is the condition of change.")*

## The Treasures

Every entity carries a **designated treasure/currency** — what it values,
hoards, and trades. Fire's treasure is revealed light; Air's is silence;
Neutron's is inertia; Dust's is formlessness; Time's is duration; Spirit's is
the shared register. The player's treasure is set by alignment.

The blindness is economic as well as perceptual: you can only perceive and
spend your own treasure. **Time is the only entity that trades between all
currencies** — it is the trader that makes the economy of the plane possible.
The **windows** are each entity's treasure-hoard, displayed as art.

*(Full mapping in `05-treasures.md`.)*

## The Systems

Every entity also exposes a **system the player can interact with** — the
treasury is *what* each holds; the system is *how* the player engages it.
Fire = Illumination, Air = Concealment/Repulsion, Neutron = Wreckage, Dust =
Building, Time = Trade/Narration, Spirit = Harmony. Each is its own system,
per the rule "new concepts get new systems." The windows are the surfaces of
these systems; the player constructs the reality by acting through them.

*(Full mapping in `06-systems.md`.)*

## The Quantum Fabric

Beneath all of this is the **quantum fabric / strings** — the substrate the
elements are patterns of, the medium the souls vibrate on. The hex grid is its
visible lattice; the liquid wave is its living vibration. The souls are
different vibration modes of the same strings; Spirit is the resonance when
Fire and Air meet; Time is the fabric's own extension. Monochrome is the
fabric when neither mode sees the other; color is its resonance made visible.

*(Full mapping in `07-fabric.md`.)*

## The Star Map

The plane is a **moving system around a star**. Light comes from directions,
not from everywhere. The Fire (Illumination) system is a **directional-light
map**: entities in space amplify or diminish the light that sweeps them. The
light is an **aftershock** — the resonance of how it was before Time traded
the sand and breathed time into clay.

*(Full mapping in `08-star-map.md`.)*

## Earth — the third playable element

Beneath Light and Dark, there is **Earth** — the clay Time gave life. Earth
holds; it is the stable ground the forces engulf. The player may align as
Earth and play **against the engulfing** — resisting, enduring, being the
ground the war is fought over.

*(Full mapping in `09-earth.md`.)*

## The Galaxy

The plane is a **galaxy** — the star-map is one local system. The player is
concentrated on **our part**, but the galaxy is vast: **leeway** for more, other
systems, other wars — all instances of the same eternal conflict. **Maps are
integral** (hex tile → star-map → galactic map); **perspective is all** — the
windows are the perspectives onto the plane.

*(Full mapping in `10-galaxy.md`.)*

## Time, Death, and the Missing Scales

When time is born, death starts beating — **death is the depth of the drum.**
Time, death, and movement persist, each on its own pendulum swing. The
**tehtnica (scales) are still missing** — the balance that would weigh duration
fairly. The justice of duration belongs to everyone and at once to no one. A
**second paradox** — the justice of duration being everyone's and no one's — is
what breaks reality when it finally arrives.
