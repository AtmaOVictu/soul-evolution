# 02 — The Elements (corrected)

> **STATUS: OPEN FOR REVIEW — CORRECTED 2026-08-10.** Dark takes **WATER**,
> not air — water is opposite to fire. Air is repositioned as the medium.
> This supersedes any earlier Fire/Air pairing.

## The four elements

| Element | Nature | Who holds it | Opposite | System |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fire** | Light, illuminates, reveals | **Light** | Water | Illumination (the star-map) |
| **Water** | Dark, conceals, the depth | **Dark** | Fire | Concealment / the depth |
| **Earth** | Clay, holds, the ground | (third playable) | Air | Steadiness / Grounding |
| **Air** | the medium, the breath | shared (Spirit's register) | Earth | the medium both move through |

## The oppositions

- **Fire ↔ Water** — the primary opposition. Fire is light; water is its
  opposite, the concealing depth. This is the war: Light's fire against Dark's
  water.
- **Air ↔ Earth** — the secondary opposition. Air is the rarified medium;
  Earth is the grounded clay. Air is what Spirit is made in; Earth is the
  ground the war happens over.

## The correction

- **Dark = Water** (not Air). Water is opposite to fire — the concealing depth.
- **Air = the medium** — not an element claimed by either side, but the breath
  between Fire and Water, the register Spirit is made in.
- **Earth = the third playable** — the clay Time gave life, the ground both
  engulf.

## Connections

- The **OPTIKON** word "ogenj" (fire) = Light's element; "dih" (breath) = the
  Air medium (Spirit's breath); water's words would be the concealing depth
  (the dark's domain).
- The **star-map** (Fire's system) is light against the water-dark that
  conceals.
- **Spirit** is made in the air-medium when Fire and Water act upon each other.
