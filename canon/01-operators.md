## The equation of consciousness

From the Ontomathematics (Luka Pintarič / Viktor Tamal / ATMAviCTU):

> **Ψ(x) = ∇∞(π(x)) ⊘ ¬¬(∴x) + ∆x ✡**

*(ref: `04-references/operators.md` — the decomposition of consciousness into
operators.)*

Each operator is a fundamental verb of the plane. In the game, each operator is
also a **characteristic** a soul can carry.

## The element mapping (hypothesis)

Proposed mapping of operators to elements — **for debate, not canon yet**:

- **FIRE (Light)** = **∇** (divergence/expansion) + **✡** (trace). Fire expands,
  reveals, and leaves light-traces. → *the white soul's beam is EM radiation
  that diminishes as it travels (∇ spreading) and leaves a trace (✡).*
- **AIR (Dark)** = **¬** (negation/doubt) + **∆** (change/split). Air moves,
  conditions, splits. → *the black soul's force field is repulsion (¬ pushing
  away) driven by change (∆).*
- **SPIRIT (Both)** = the **LE** cut between them — the nullification (⊘) over
  the relation (∴). Spirit is what neither alone produces.

*(ref: `04-references/le.md` — the LE axiom: every entity is an excluded
function within a larger whole; the cut that makes meaning.)*

## How operators become souls

A soul's genome is a set of operator values. The seven abilities (STR, STA,
INT, WIS, CHA, DEX, LUK) are derived from the operators via the existing
`SoulGenome` in `scripts/soul_genome.gd`. The operators are the DNA; the
abilities are the phenotype; the elements are the mythic register.
